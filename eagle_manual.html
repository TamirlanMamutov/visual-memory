How to read it

The red box is EAGLE's answer: the frames where it last saw the object, and where on screen. On the timeline, the blue line is the model's confidence on frames where it found something; the red bar is its answer and the green bar the ground truth. With a VQ2D query left unchanged, the result matches that checkpoint's saved evaluation. tIoU and stIoU follow the official VQ2D definitions; found means stIoU ≥ 0.05.
